Our Story

Care X Nursing was founded to provide expert skilled nursing services with compassion, professionalism, and reliability.

As a licensed skilled nursing agency, we deliver advanced medical care outside of the hospital setting. Our highly trained nurses specialize in wound care, medication administration, chronic disease management, post-surgical recovery, and other essential clinical services. We are committed to improving patient outcomes and enhancing quality of life through personalized care plans.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does your nursing agency provide?

We offer a full range of skilled nursing services, including medication management, wound care, IV therapy, rehabilitation support, and assistance with activities of daily living, all delivered by licensed professionals.

How do I schedule care for my loved one?

Scheduling is simple — you can call our office, send an email, or fill out our online contact form. Our care coordinator will review your needs and arrange an assessment at a time convenient for you.

Are your nurses licensed and trained?

Yes. All our nurses and caregivers are fully licensed, background-checked, and receive ongoing training to ensure they provide the highest standard of compassionate, professional care.

Do you provide care for people with complex medical needs?

Absolutely. Our team is experienced in handling a wide range of medical conditions, including post-surgical care, chronic illnesses, and rehabilitation support, while tailoring care plans to each individual’s requirements.

How is the cost of services determined?

Costs depend on the type and level of care required. After an initial assessment, we provide a detailed care plan outlining services, frequency, and associated costs, ensuring transparency and no surprises.

Can care be provided in my home or only at a facility?

We offer both in-home care and facility-based services. Our team works closely with families to choose the setting that best meets the client’s medical and personal needs.
